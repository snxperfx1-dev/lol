## 3. Architectural Laws (normative — MUST / MUST NOT)

### L1 — Master Integration Law
F60 **IS NOT** the final algorithm. F60 is the foundational substrate. Its engines
(`f_phys`, `f_se`, Engine 1A, recursive curve tree, FCE, Invisible Network, Time
Intelligence, fractal stack) **feed** the higher layers. They **MUST NOT** replace them.

### L2 — Upward-Only Information Flow
The canonical pipeline (§5) flows **upward only**.
- **MUST NOT** let any layer *bypass* a lower layer.
- **MUST NOT** let any layer *recompute* what a lower layer already produces authoritatively.
- **MUST NOT** let any layer *delete* a lower layer.
- A higher layer reads lower-layer outputs; it never reaches around them to raw price.

### L3 — Non-Competition Law
`ERF, FRZ, RIE, MCE, NE, TQE, TE, IE2, WR, DWR, DOE, Senseei` are **NOT alternatives** to F60
or to each other. They are **successive, orthogonal observers** — each answers a question no
other layer answers (see §2 and §6). Treating any of them as a "competing engine" to be
chosen-between or deleted is **prohibited**.

### L4 — F60 Enrichment Law
F60 modules are **superior information producers** and **force multipliers**, not replacements.
- Every legacy/V72 engine **MUST** be upgraded to consume authentic F60 outputs where an
  authoritative F60 equivalent exists.
- **No V72 engine may keep using a proxy calculation when an authentic F60 source exists.**
- The proxy→authentic substitutions in §6 are **mandatory**.

### L5 — Preservation Rule
If a module existed in Omega **and has a downstream consumer, it MUST remain.**
A module may be removed **only if ALL** of the following hold:
1. it has **no consumers**, **and**
2. it **duplicates** a lower-layer authoritative calculation, **and**
3. keeping it would **violate** an authority law (L1–L4).
Otherwise: **preserve it** (and enrich its inputs per L4).

### L6 — Senseei Is Not The Brain
Senseei is a presentation / meta-interpretation layer. It answers *"given all engines, what is
happening?"* — **not** *"what should reality be?"*.
- Senseei **MUST NOT** be the central brain, the canonical authority, the execution controller,
  or the master decision engine.
- **No other layer may depend on Senseei.** Deleting Senseei must leave system intelligence
  fully intact.
- Senseei may be replaced, upgraded, or removed entirely (e.g. by HyperIntelligence).

### L7 — Canonical Decision Authority = DOE (or HyperIntelligence)
The canonical machine decision is produced by **DOE** (or a successor **HyperIntelligence**
synthesis engine), which **synthesizes** the observer stack and produces
`doe_bias · doe_action · doe_confidence · doe_tradeType · doe_entry`.
- DOE **computes no physics, no phases, no structure** — it only synthesizes.
- Removing DOE is **forbidden**. Senseei does not replace DOE; Senseei *interprets* DOE.

### L8 — Prohibition of Collapsed Builds
The following deliverables are **forbidden** and are to be treated as failures:
- a pure F60 file; a standalone Senseei file;
- a stripped/"simplified core algo"; an "equivalent implementation";
- any single-file rewrite that omits ERF/FRZ/RIE/MCE/NE/TQE/TE/IE2/WR/DWR/DOE or the
  F60 recursive tree / FCE / Invisible Network / chain vitality / compression persistence.

   Execution still compile and decide. (Then restore Senseei.)
